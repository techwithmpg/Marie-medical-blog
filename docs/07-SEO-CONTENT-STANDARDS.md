# 07 — SEO & Medical Content Standards

## SEO is part of V1
Public pages must be built to be indexable, semantic, performant, and shareable.

## Article URL
`/blog/[slug]`

Slugs should be:
- human readable;
- lower-case;
- hyphenated;
- stable after publication where possible;
- unique.

## Metadata
Support:
- title
- description
- canonical URL
- Open Graph title/description/image
- relevant Twitter/social metadata

## Structured data
Use appropriate schema such as Article or BlogPosting when the content and current search-engine guidance support it. Do not add misleading medical credential schema.

## Sitemap/robots
- generated sitemap includes public canonical pages and published articles;
- drafts/admin/private routes are not indexed;
- robots configuration aligns with actual access/indexing intent.

## Content credibility
Article pages should visibly support:
- author context;
- publication date;
- last updated date when updated;
- category/topic;
- references;
- medical disclaimer.

## Portfolio SEO
Portfolio pages should demonstrate expertise without duplicating complete article content unnecessarily.

## Search Console launch tasks
- connect verified production domain;
- submit sitemap;
- inspect representative article URLs;
- check indexing and metadata after deployment.

## Content safety rule
Development agents do not invent authoritative medical claims merely to fill the site. Use clearly marked sample/placeholder content until Marie supplies reviewed articles.
