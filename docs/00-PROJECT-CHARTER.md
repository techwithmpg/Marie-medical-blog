# 00 — Project Charter

## Product
Marie Medere Medical Writing Portfolio & Educational Blog.

## Primary audience
- potential medical-writing employers and clients;
- general public seeking understandable health education;
- student doctors;
- doctors who may share educational links with patients where appropriate.

## Primary outcomes
1. Marie has a credible, professional online body of work.
2. Marie can publish and manage content independently.
3. Readers can find, read, search, and share medical education content easily.
4. Employers can quickly identify Marie's strongest writing samples and contact her.

## Product positioning
A professional personal medical publication and writing portfolio, not a generic CMS and not a healthcare delivery system.

## V1 success criteria
- portfolio-quality public site;
- excellent mobile article reading;
- simple private publishing dashboard;
- credible referencing/disclaimer structure;
- strong SEO foundation;
- reliable deployment and secure data access;
- no unnecessary enterprise complexity.

## Commercial boundary
The frozen V1 represents the agreed initial project value. New workflows/modules are change requests, not implicit additions.
